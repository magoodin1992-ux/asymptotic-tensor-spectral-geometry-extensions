**[SRG-7C91 — A zero spectral marginal with positive asymptotic wire cost](SRG-7C91.md)**

A unit equilateral triangle and its centroid provide an exact separation between the mean-width spectral price of a shared label and its asymptotically realizable cost in a fixed four-site pair-wire tensor restriction model. The spectral price remains 3/2 per bit, while demanding the centroid raises the optimal asymptotic wire cost from 3/2 to 1 + 1/√3. The note includes rank-cut lower bounds and matching constructive upper bounds, with source attribution and explicit limitations.
